Projects

Sep 2025 – present

BioFlow

Technical lead, AI-for-science platform at Peking Union Medical College Hospital

An internal platform that lets researchers build metagenomic analyses as graphs and get reproducible results and figures. It's used by about 50 researchers and has supported 30+ papers and projects. I'm one of two technical leads on the 40-person team and lead the data-analysis and skills modules (about 20 engineers), owning their system design and deployment.

The core is a DAG workflow engine: graphs are validated at compile time, compiled to Nextflow, cached by content so only invalidated nodes rerun, and every figure keeps a link to the run that produced it, so generated papers can only cite computed results.

FastAPI, React, TypeScript, Nextflow, Qwen3-14B (local)

Dec 2025 – May 2026

NutriWise

RAG-based digital textbook on child nutrition (undergraduate thesis)

A question-answering textbook with separate student and teacher modes, used by 800+ elementary school students through a deployment on their school's servers. Answers are grounded in reading materials written with nutrition experts at Peking Union Medical College Hospital, curated health articles, and 610 high-impact journal papers, and every answer lists its sources down to the PubMed ID.

In a blind evaluation, it outscored DeepSeek and Kimi on child-friendliness (4.9 vs. at most 3.3 out of 5) and traceability (4.8 vs. at most 2.2 out of 5).

Separately from the school deployment, I also deployed it to AWS EC2 with Docker Compose (app, Redis, and Caddy), automatic HTTPS, and streamed answers. GitHub Actions runs the pytest suite and Ruff on the open-source repo.

Python, Flask, LangChain, FAISS, BGE embeddings, server-sent events, Docker, AWS EC2, GitHub Actions

In progress

LexAsk

A legal agent that asks before it answers

Most legal assistants answer whatever they're given. LexAsk tracks which legal elements of a case are still unknown and uses a sufficiency scorer to decide when to ask, what to ask, and when to stop. I'm designing the scorer and training it into Qwen3-8B with SFT and multi-turn GRPO against simulated clients, with deterministic rewards that are hard to game.

Research Assistant, advised by Prof. Weijie Yu

I work on model editing and interpretability for legal AI. Language models asked to judge criminal cases can keep a guilty verdict even after a legally decisive fact changes.
