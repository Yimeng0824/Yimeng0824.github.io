01 / About

Hello, I'm Yimeng Wang.

My research interests lie at the intersection of natural language processing and reliable AI. I am particularly interested in how language models make decisions under changes to decisive evidence, and how to improve their behavior while preserving useful capabilities.

02 / Research Interests

What I'm exploring

Trustworthy Language Models

Evaluating model reliability, failure modes, and robustness across changing evidence.

Legal Natural Language Processing

Language technologies for legal reasoning, case understanding, and charge verification.

Model Editing

Targeted behavior correction with attention to preservation and unintended changes.
